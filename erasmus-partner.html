Why Erasmus+?

The real benefits of Erasmus+ participation

Erasmus+ is far more than a funded trip abroad. It is a structured investment in the professional growth of your staff and the long-term development of your institution.

Professional growth

New skills, new perspectives

Participants return with concrete methods, tools and ideas that they can apply immediately in their own classroom, office or community setting.

EU funding

Fully or largely funded mobility

Erasmus+ grants cover travel, accommodation, daily allowance and course fees, making international professional development accessible to all organisations.

European network

International connections

Every mobility is an opportunity to build lasting partnerships with peers from across Europe — contacts that often lead to future collaborative projects.

Institutional impact

Stronger organisations

Staff who participate in mobility bring back energy, motivation and fresh approaches. Institutions consistently report higher engagement and improved practice after Erasmus+ activities.

Recognition

Certified learning

Participants receive official documentation of their learning — Europass Mobility supplements, certificates and attendance records — recognised across EU member states.

European dimension

A broader vision for education

Erasmus+ embeds a European and intercultural perspective in your institution's culture — supporting inclusion, diversity and open-minded professional thinking.

How it works

From first contact to successful mobility

Initial contact
